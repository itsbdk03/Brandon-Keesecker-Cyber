# Security and responsible use

**Educational software, not an assessment or penetration-testing service.** All sample datasets and scenarios are synthetic; findings are illustrative and do not establish compliance with NIST, FedRAMP, DoD IL, SOC 2, HIPAA, HITRUST or another framework.

Do not submit secrets, authentication tokens, customer records, CUI, PHI, or other sensitive material. The example analyses execute locally in your browser, but public websites are not an approved channel for sensitive documents.

The rule engines intentionally cover a limited JSON schema and fictional graph. Unknown or missing inputs must not be considered safe. No live scanning, exploitation, cloud-account access, or customer data collection is performed.

If you find a vulnerability in a public copy, contact the website owner privately instead of disclosing sensitive details in a public issue.
